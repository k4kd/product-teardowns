# PM lens

This file says which product decisions are worth the reader's time, through build-products-pm-kunal's view: what someone building a product has to get right to find users, keep them and make money. Only include rows where the evidence found something. Leave a row out when there's none, and never guess to fill the table.

**Verdicts:**
- **Steal:** a decision the reader can copy, **with its conditions** ("works when usage is weekly or more and serving a free user costs under a cent").
- **Skip:** a decision that only works at their scale or in their market, or that the evidence suggests is hurting them. Say what to do instead.

Deep ideas worth learning properly are not a verdict. They become new concepts in the doc.

**Red flags to call out plainly:** dark patterns (hidden costs, forced continuity, hard-to-cancel), growth that depends on one channel they don't control, a free tier with no path to paying, and pricing that punishes the users who get the most value. Describe each as a lesson, with evidence.

## Smallest version you could build

End with one paragraph: if the reader built this business today, what's the least that would work? Which user, which job, which one loop, which price, and which one or two concepts would they need to master?
